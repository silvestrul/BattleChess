# The Great Field - forty thousand worth a side.
#
# The largest order of battle in this project: twenty regiments an army, every
# one of them two thousand worth and every one of them the same rectangle,
# 80 x 40 m, on ground with room for all of it.
#
#   3 cavalry        700 men each   on the wings, where horse is worth having
#   2 horse archers  800 men each   outside them again, free to swing
#   8 spearmen      2000 men each   the front line
#   7 swordsmen     1600 men each   the second line
#  -----------------------------
#  20 regiments    40 000 worth     each side, identical
#
# The infantry split is eight spear regiments to seven sword rather than an even
# division: fifteen regiments cannot halve. Change the eight to seven and the
# seven to eight to lean the other way.
#
# EVERY REGIMENT COVERS THE SAME GROUND, which is the designer's rule and the
# whole point of costPerMan in units.cfg. A regiment is two thousand *worth*,
# not two thousand *men*: a horseman costs 2,86 footmen, so seven hundred riders
# fill the rectangle that two thousand spearmen fill. This file used to ask for
# two thousand of everything and raise its own maxStrength to allow it, which
# put 229 x 114 m of cavalry on the field beside 80 x 40 m of spearmen - horse
# nearly three times the frontage of foot, and every measurement taken here
# inherited the distortion. See M133 and BattleGroundTests.
#
# Both armies stand about a kilometre apart, in the clear, with their northern
# flank on a range of small mountains and their southern on the river. The two
# knolls in between are a march from either start line.
#
# Positions are authored map cells (column,row), matching what `map greatfield`
# draws, and were computed from the built footprints rather than estimated:
# nothing here overlaps at deployment.

name        = The Great Field
map         = greatfield
seed        = 20260818
