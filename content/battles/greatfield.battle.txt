# The Great Field - forty thousand a side.
#
# The largest order of battle in this project, and built to be exactly that:
# twenty regiments an army at two thousand men each, eighty thousand on the
# field between them, on ground with room for all of it.
#
#   3 cavalry        6 000    on the wings, where horse is worth having
#   2 horse archers  4 000    outside them again, free to swing
#   8 spearmen      16 000    the front line
#   7 swordsmen     14 000    the second line
#  ------------------------
#  20 regiments     40 000    each side, identical
#
# The infantry split is 16 000 spears to 14 000 swords rather than an even
# 15 000 apiece: fifteen regiments cannot halve, and holding every regiment to
# exactly two thousand was worth more than splitting the difference. Change the
# eight to seven and the seven to eight to lean the other way.
#
# TWO THOUSAND IS NOT A NORMAL REGIMENT. units.cfg caps cavalry at 700 and
# horse archers at 800, for the good reason that a two-thousand-strong body of
# horse is 229 m across its front and 114 m deep - a quarter of a kilometre of
# cavalry in one rectangle. This battle raises its own ceiling rather than
# editing the shared catalogue, so nothing else in content inherits it. What it
# looks like on the field is worth watching before it is copied.
#
# Both armies stand about a kilometre apart, in the clear, with their northern
# flank on a range of small mountains and their southern on the river. The two
# knolls in between are a march from either start line.
#
# Positions are authored map cells (column,row), matching what `map greatfield`
# draws, and were computed from the built footprints rather than estimated:
# nothing here overlaps at deployment.

name        = The Great Field
map         = greatfield
seed        = 20260818
