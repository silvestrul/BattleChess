# Melee — a test scenario for the damage rule.
#
# Pairs deployed 30 m apart, already touching, so combat begins immediately and
# nothing has to be manoeuvred first.
#
#   0 Spearmen  vs  1 Swordsmen   the even line fight the rate is calibrated on
#   2 Cavalry   vs  3 Archers     what cavalry is for
#   4 Cavalry   vs  5 Spearmen    what cavalry must never do
#   6 Spearmen  vs  7 Spearmen    600 against 200: three to one, and even
#
# The last pair is the one worth watching. Both present 30 m of contact, so both
# fight with the same number of men, and the extra 400 stand idle. That is why
# envelopment exists.
#
# Try:  fight melee 0 1     fight melee 2 3     fight melee 6 7
