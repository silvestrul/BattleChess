# Orders — `O`

What an order means once given, and how it ends.

| | Requirement | Priority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | Attack orders align centres — the attacker's middle goes for the defender's middle. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| O2 | Archers and horse archers told to attack stop at shooting range. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| O3 | Defenders do not turn to face attackers unless told to. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| O4 | Pursuit only when ordered to attack. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| O5 | Two attackers on one face go for the centre, then share it. | Mandatory | ❌ new |
