# Combat — `C`

How a fight is arranged, resolved and paid for.

| | Requirement | Priority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Two arrangements only: square on to the enemy's front, or perpendicular against a flank. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C2 | Flanking is earned by getting round the side first, not by approaching at an angle. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C3 | Two regiments to a face, four faces. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C4 | Overflow is **never** reassigned to another face. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C5 | Frontage is refilled from reserves. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C6 | A face is worth **one full frontage** however many press it. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C7 | What a second regiment buys is the defender's nerve, not his blood — more morale inflicted, less taken for having company. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C8 | The first rank fights fully; ranks 2–3 contribute less; the rest resupply the front. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C9 | A dead soldier is never replaced. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C10 | Cavalry breaks through infantry, but never through cavalry and never through spearmen. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C11 | A regiment can withdraw from a fight unless fully encircled, taking casualties in proportion to how much of it is gripped. | Mandatory | ❌ finding 2 |
| C12 | Tiredness runs 0 to 1, up to −75% damage, worst in the front rank, with some accrued for waiting under stress. | Mandatory | ❌ #42 |
| C13 | Ranks refill the front rank. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C14 | Casualties must not round away. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C15 | A flank engages a side, not a frontage. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C16 | Flanking is shock, and shock wears off. | Mandatory | ❌ new |
| C17 | Turning to face cancels the shock outright. | Mandatory | ❌ new |
| C18 | A **shallow** regiment taken in the flank suffers worse **morale**, not worse casualties. | Preferred | ❌ new, threshold to settle |
| C19 | Four sides, and each side knows how many men stand along it. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C19a | Holes in the line are charged as a share of the face, not as a count of men. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C19b | Enveloping brings twice the face, off the same lookup. | Mandatory | ✅ |
| C20 | A rear attack is a flank attack that is full width. | Mandatory | ❌ needs C16 |
| C20a | `OutOfArcPenalty` belongs to C20 and is currently in the wrong place. | Mandatory | ❌ with C20 |
